## 4. High-value points other candidates usually miss

Use these as “wow inserts” during demo or Q&A. Each is a mini-thesis.

### 4.1 Relative ranking beats absolute scores
A 72 in crowded AI infra Series B is different from a 72 in an empty robotics subsector.  
Signal ranks **within theme × stage**, not on a fake universal IQ score.

> “Absolute scores create false precision. Relative rank creates useful debate.”

### 4.2 Portfolio mix as a first-class constraint (60/40)
Most tools score companies in isolation. Thirdbase explicitly wants **60% dominant tech/growth** and **40% tactical**.  
Signal can answer: *Are we overweight tactical right now?* That is partner-grade, not chatbot-grade.

### 4.3 Selectivity is the product
Hard caps on digests (3–5 deals, 1–2 sector calls, 3–5 reads) are not a limitation — they are the design.  
If the digest is long, **Signal failed**, not the partner.

> “If they won’t forward it, we failed.”

### 4.4 Stale ≠ delete
Auto-deleting pipeline entries is how you lose institutional memory and piss off partners.  
Flag ≥90 days for **partner review**. Human-in-the-loop on destructive actions.

### 4.5 Thesis is config, not a prompt
Putting criteria in `thesis_policy.yaml` means:
- Auditable  
- Diffable in git  
- Editable without redeploying “the AI’s personality”  
- Survivable when models change  

Other candidates: “the system prompt says we like AI.”  
You: “the firm’s investment constitution is version-controlled.”

### 4.6 Excel is a feature (say it with pride)
Fighting partner culture is how internal tools die.  
Debate happens in sheets. Signal regenerates the workbook as the **shared object of argument**.

### 4.7 Provenance > eloquence
A beautiful wrong brief is worse than a plain correct one.  
Every material field should carry confidence + source. Never invent private valuations as fact.

### 4.8 Peer intelligence is alpha-adjacent
Tracking a16z / Sequoia / Lux / etc. is not gossip — it is:
- Syndicate mapping  
- Thesis-shift detection (off-stated-focus bets)  
- Crowding risk on Deep Dives  
- White-space vs flood themes  

Position peer OS as **competitive awareness**, not voyeurism.

### 4.9 Commentary often beats the round size
Qualitative signal (engineers love it / customers churning / GPs privately skeptical) is frequently more decision-relevant than headline funding.  
Surface commentary with links; don’t drown partners in sentiment dashboards.

### 4.10 Sector of Tomorrow must be contrarian *and* evidenced
“AI is hot” is worthless.  
“Three AI infra subsectors with GP chatter + hiring + research velocity before consensus” is investable curiosity.

### 4.11 Special routing > batch everything
2+ Tier-1 co-invest, off-thesis peer move, watched founder — these should not wait for Wednesday.  
Shows you understand **asymmetric urgency** in venture.

### 4.12 Anti-goals are leadership
State what you refuse:
- Authenticated scraping of Blind / LinkedIn / X without vendors  
- Training a foundation model on 500 deals  
- Replacing partner taste  
- Infinite unranked lists  

Non-goals signal maturity. Junior candidates promise everything.

### 4.13 Connectors later, judgment now
MVP with seed + EDGAR/HN/RSS is not weakness if the scoring and workflow are real.  
> “Databases are purchase orders. Judgment is the hard problem — and we solved for that first.”

### 4.14 The output is a ritual, not a repository
Success = partners open M/W/F digest without being chased.  
Failure = a clever database nobody habitates.

### 4.15 LP-readable process
LPs increasingly ask about “AI in the investment process.”  
Signal gives Thirdbase a coherent answer: encoded thesis, auditable scores, human overrides, no black-box auto-invest.

## 10. Investment epistemology — the deep layer that impresses true GPs

This section is intellectual ammunition. Use 1–2 points live; know the rest.

### 10.1 Venture is an attention market
Capital is abundant relative to partner hours. The scarce resource is **cognitive slots**.  
Signal’s job is queue discipline for those slots.

### 10.2 False positives are expensive
A wrong Deep Dive burns a week of partner/associate time and creates narrative inertia (“we’ve already spent cycles”).  
Optimize for **precision of Hot Deals**, not recall of every rumor.

### 10.3 False negatives are asymmetric by theme
Missing a deal in a white-space theme hurts more than missing the 40th AI wrapper.  
Peer white-space / flood posture helps allocate search effort.

### 10.4 Cap table is a signal and a trap
3–4 Tier-1s is preferred — also a crowding / price signal.  
Good systems show both: quality *and* competitive heat.

### 10.5 Timing is a dimension, not a vibe
“Why now” should be structured: funding event, hiring inflection, regulatory shift, founder move, peer entry — not poetic filler.

### 10.6 Commentary is a leading indicator
Price and rounds are lagging. Engineer love, customer skepticism, GP tone shifts often lead.

### 10.7 Thesis drift detection on peers
When a firm invests off stated focus, two readings:
- They know something  
- They are lost / marketing  
Either is worth a human look. Automating the *flag* is valuable; automating the *interpretation* is not.

### 10.8 The IC brief is a decision object
Funding history, Tier mix, team, traction, thesis fit, comps, commentary, recommendation — one page.  
If your system can’t produce that on demand, it’s a toy.

## 11. “Stress test” questions — and elite answers

### Q: What if the model hallucinates a Series B?
**A:** Facts come from stored signals with provenance. The model writes prose over evidence. If evidence is missing, we say unknown. Estimated valuations are labeled. Early months: human skim before digest send.

### Q: Why shouldn’t we just use PitchBook + ChatGPT Enterprise?
**A:** PitchBook is a library. ChatGPT is a pen. Neither maintains Thirdbase’s living pipeline, 60/40 mix, selective digest ritual, or peer matrix under our peer set. Signal is the operating system that binds them.

### Q: How do you know the scores are right?
**A:** We don’t claim omniscience. We claim consistency + explainability. Partners override; overrides feed policy revision. Monthly we review Hot Deal precision. The score is a debate starter with receipts.

### Q: What’s your unfair data advantage?
**A:** Short-term: none claimed — honesty. Medium-term: proprietary *labels* from Thirdbase overrides, Pass reasons, and IC outcomes. That preference data is the real firm asset — not scraped HTML.

### Q: Will this replace associates?
**A:** It replaces tab chaos and inconsistent monitoring. Associates move up to judgment, founder work, and diligence. Tools that claim replacement usually deliver mediocre coverage.

### Q: What’s the biggest risk of hiring you to build this?
**A:** Building a beautiful system partners don’t habit. That’s why success is digest adoption and Deep Dive precision — and why Excel/email are primary surfaces.

### Q: Open source vs closed models?
**A:** Use best hosted models behind a router for synthesis. Keep scoring logic and data in our control. Swap models when quality/price shifts. Don’t marry a brand.

### Q: How fast to production value?
**A:** 30 days to real M/W/F habit on current stack. 90 days to first serious paid connector once trust exists. Don’t buy six vendors before partners care.

### Q: Should we sell this?
**A:** Not until we’d be angry to lose it internally — and only if legal allows given data licenses. Selling the Thirdbase brain dilutes edge; selling a configurable judgment machine is a Year-2+ question.

### Q: What would you cut if budget is tight?
**A:** Cut fancy UI chrome and extra data vendors first. Never cut: thesis engine, digest selectivity, provenance, stale human review.
